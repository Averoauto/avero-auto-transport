Privacy Policy

AVERO Auto Transport LLC ("AVERO," "we," "us," or "our") is committed to protecting your privacy and maintaining the confidentiality of your personal information. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and share information when you access our website, submit a quote request, communicate with us, or use our vehicle transportation services.

By using our website or voluntarily providing your information, you acknowledge the practices described in this Privacy Policy.

What Personal Information We Collect

When you interact with AVERO Auto Transport LLC, we may collect personal and service-related information, including:

  • Name
  • Phone number
  • Email address
  • Vehicle information
  • Pickup and delivery locations
  • Transportation request details
  • SMS consent and opt-in information
  • Other information you voluntarily provide through our website or during communication with us

How We Collect Information

We collect information when you:

  • Submit a quote or transportation request through our website
  • Provide your contact information through a website form
  • Voluntarily opt in to receive SMS communications
  • Contact us by phone, email, or other communication methods
  • Communicate with us regarding your vehicle transportation request or services

SMS consent is obtained through an optional, unchecked SMS consent checkbox on our website quote form. Checking the SMS consent box is voluntary and is not a condition of purchasing our services.

How We Use Your Information

We may use the information we collect to:

  • Provide and coordinate vehicle transportation services
  • Respond to quote and transportation requests
  • Communicate with you regarding your transportation request
  • Send SMS messages when you have voluntarily opted in to receive them
  • Provide pickup and delivery updates
  • Communicate regarding scheduling and transportation status
  • Provide customer support
  • Respond to questions and inquiries
  • Maintain and improve our website and services
  • Comply with applicable legal and regulatory requirements

SMS Communications

If you voluntarily check the SMS consent box on our website quote form, you agree to receive SMS messages from AVERO Auto Transport LLC regarding your vehicle transportation request.

SMS messages may include:

  • Quotes and quote-related communications
  • Scheduling information
  • Pickup and delivery updates
  • Transportation status updates
  • Customer support communications

Message frequency may vary. Message and data rates may apply.

You may opt out of SMS communications at any time by replying STOP to any message. For assistance, reply HELP.

Your consent to receive SMS messages is not a condition of purchasing any goods or services from AVERO Auto Transport LLC.

How We Share Your Information

AVERO Auto Transport LLC does not sell or rent your personal information.

We may use or share information when necessary to provide and coordinate the requested vehicle transportation services. For example, certain information may be provided to transportation partners, carriers, brokers, or other service providers when necessary to arrange or complete a transportation request.

We do not share your personal information with third parties for their own marketing purposes.

SMS opt-in information and phone numbers collected for SMS communications are not shared or sold to third parties or affiliates for their own marketing purposes.

We may also disclose information when required by law, legal process, or to protect our rights, customers, or services.

Data Security

We take reasonable measures to protect the personal information submitted to us from unauthorized access, use, disclosure, or misuse. However, no method of transmitting or storing information electronically can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

Your Choices

You may choose not to provide certain information; however, some information may be necessary for us to respond to your transportation request or provide requested services.

For SMS communications, you may withdraw your consent at any time by replying STOP to an SMS message. You may also contact us directly regarding your SMS preferences.

Changes to This Privacy Policy

AVERO Auto Transport LLC may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be posted on this page with an updated effective date when applicable.
