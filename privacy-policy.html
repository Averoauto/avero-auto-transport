Privacy Policy

Effective Date: September 9, 2026

1. Information We Collect

AVERO Auto Transport LLC ("AVERO," "we," "us," or "our") may collect personal information that customers, brokers, dealerships, carriers, and transportation partners provide to us.

This information may include names, phone numbers, mobile phone numbers, email addresses, pickup and delivery locations, vehicle information, transportation details, and other information reasonably necessary to provide and coordinate vehicle transportation services.

2. How We Use Personal Information

We use personal information to provide and coordinate vehicle transportation services, communicate with customers and business partners, respond to inquiries, provide quotes, schedule pickups and deliveries, coordinate transportation orders, provide customer support, and communicate information related to requested services.

3. Sharing of Personal Information

AVERO may share personal information when reasonably necessary to provide or coordinate requested vehicle transportation services.

For example, information may be shared with carriers, brokers, dealerships, transportation partners, and service providers when necessary to arrange, perform, or complete a transportation service requested by the customer.

Mobile Phone Numbers and SMS Opt-In Information:

AVERO Auto Transport LLC does not sell, rent, or share mobile phone numbers or SMS opt-in information with third parties or affiliates for their own marketing, advertising, or promotional purposes.

Mobile phone numbers and SMS consent information will not be used by third parties to send their own marketing or promotional messages.

AVERO will only use mobile phone numbers and SMS consent information for communications related to AVERO's services, customer support, requested transportation services, and other communications authorized by the customer.

4. SMS Communications

If you voluntarily provide your mobile phone number and separately consent to receive SMS messages from AVERO, we may send text messages related to your vehicle transportation request or services.

SMS messages may include quotes, quote follow-ups, scheduling information, pickup and delivery updates, appointment confirmations, driver or transportation updates, responses to customer inquiries, customer support, and other information related to an active transportation request or service.

Message frequency may vary depending on your interactions with AVERO and the transportation services requested.

Message and data rates may apply depending on your mobile carrier and service plan.

You may opt out of SMS communications at any time by replying STOP. After sending STOP, you will no longer receive SMS messages from AVERO unless you provide consent again.

For assistance with SMS communications, reply HELP or contact AVERO using the contact information provided on our website.

SMS consent is not a condition of purchasing any goods or services from AVERO Auto Transport LLC.

5. SMS Consent and Opt-In

Customers may provide SMS consent through the AVERO Auto Transport website by voluntarily selecting the SMS consent checkbox on the vehicle transportation quote request form.

The SMS consent checkbox is optional, is not pre-selected, and requires an affirmative action by the customer. Submitting a quote request without selecting the SMS consent checkbox does not constitute consent to receive SMS messages.

By selecting the SMS consent checkbox, the customer expressly agrees to receive SMS messages from AVERO Auto Transport LLC at the mobile phone number provided.

Customers may withdraw SMS consent at any time by replying STOP.

6. Data Security

AVERO takes reasonable measures to protect personal information from unauthorized access, use, alteration, or disclosure.

However, no method of electronic transmission or electronic storage can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

7. Data Retention

AVERO retains personal information, including applicable SMS consent records, for as long as reasonably necessary to provide services, maintain business records, document customer communications and consent, comply with legal obligations, resolve disputes, and enforce our agreements.

8. Cookies and Website Information

Our website may use standard technologies necessary for website functionality, security, and basic operation. We do not use cookies or similar technologies to sell or share mobile phone numbers or SMS opt-in information for third-party marketing purposes.

9. Changes to This Privacy Policy

AVERO may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be posted on this page with an updated effective date.
