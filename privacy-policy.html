Privacy Policy

Effective Date: September 9, 2026

1. Information We Collect

AVERO Auto Transport LLC ("AVERO," "we," "us," or "our") may collect personal information that customers, brokers, dealerships, carriers, and transportation partners provide to us. This information may include names, phone numbers, email addresses, pickup and delivery locations, vehicle information, and other information necessary to provide and coordinate vehicle transportation services.

2. How We Use Personal Information

We use personal information to provide and coordinate vehicle transportation services, communicate with customers and business partners, schedule pickups and deliveries, provide customer support, coordinate transportation orders, and respond to inquiries.

3. Sharing of Personal Information

We may share necessary personal information with carriers, brokers, dealerships, transportation partners, service providers, and other parties when reasonably necessary to provide or coordinate requested vehicle transportation services.

SMS consent and mobile phone numbers will not be shared with third parties or affiliates for their own marketing purposes.

4. SMS Communications

If you provide your mobile phone number and consent to receive SMS messages from AVERO, we may send text messages related to vehicle transportation services and customer support.

SMS messages may include pickup and delivery updates, scheduling information, appointment confirmations, transportation-related information, responses to inquiries, and other communications related to an active transportation service or request.

Message frequency may vary depending on your interactions with AVERO and the transportation services requested.

Message and data rates may apply depending on your mobile carrier and service plan.

You may opt out of SMS communications at any time by replying STOP. For assistance, reply HELP.

SMS consent is not a condition of purchasing any goods or services from AVERO Auto Transport LLC.

5. Data Security

AVERO takes reasonable measures to protect personal information from unauthorized access, use, alteration, or disclosure. However, no method of electronic transmission or storage can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

6. Data Retention

We retain personal information for as long as reasonably necessary to provide our services, maintain business records, comply with legal obligations, resolve disputes, and enforce our agreements.

7. Changes to This Privacy Policy

AVERO may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be posted on this page with an updated effective date.
