SMS Terms & Conditions

Last Updated: September 2026

By voluntarily checking the SMS consent checkbox on the AVERO Auto Transport LLC website quote form, you agree to receive SMS messages from AVERO Auto Transport LLC.

1. SMS Program

AVERO Auto Transport LLC may send SMS messages related to your vehicle transportation request.

Messages may include:

  • Vehicle transportation quotes
  • Scheduling information
  • Pickup and delivery updates
  • Transportation status updates
  • Customer support communications

2. Message Frequency

Message frequency may vary depending on your transportation request and communications with AVERO.

3. Message and Data Rates

Message and data rates may apply depending on your mobile carrier and wireless plan.

4. Opt-Out

You may opt out of SMS messages at any time by replying STOP to any message from AVERO.

After sending STOP, you will receive a confirmation message and will no longer receive SMS messages from AVERO unless you choose to opt in again.

5. Help

For help, reply HELP to an AVERO SMS message or contact us directly at:

averotransport@gmail.com

6. Consent

SMS consent is voluntary and is not a condition of purchasing goods or services from AVERO Auto Transport LLC.

By checking the SMS consent checkbox, you provide your express written consent to receive SMS messages at the phone number provided on the quote form.

7. Privacy

Your use of the AVERO SMS program is also subject to our Privacy Policy.
