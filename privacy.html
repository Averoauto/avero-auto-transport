Privacy Policy

Last Updated: September 2026

AVERO Auto Transport LLC ("AVERO," "we," "us," or "our") respects your privacy and is committed to protecting the personal information you provide when using our website and requesting vehicle transportation services.

1. Information We Collect

When you submit a quote request or contact us, we may collect information such as:

  • Full name
  • Phone number
  • Email address
  • Vehicle information
  • Pickup and delivery locations
  • Transportation preferences
  • Additional information you voluntarily provide

2. How We Collect Information

We collect information when you voluntarily provide it through our website quote form, contact methods, email, phone calls, or other direct communications with AVERO.

3. How We Use Your Information

We may use the information you provide to:

  • Respond to quote requests
  • Contact you regarding your transportation request
  • Coordinate vehicle pickup and delivery
  • Provide transportation status updates
  • Provide customer support
  • Communicate with transportation partners when necessary to arrange requested services

4. SMS Communications

If you voluntarily check the SMS consent checkbox on our website, you expressly consent to receive SMS messages from AVERO Auto Transport LLC regarding your vehicle transportation request.

SMS messages may include quotes, scheduling information, pickup and delivery updates, transportation status information, and customer support communications.

Message frequency may vary. Message and data rates may apply. Reply STOP to opt out or HELP for assistance.

SMS consent is optional and is not a condition of purchasing goods or services from AVERO.

SMS opt-in information and phone numbers collected for SMS communications are not sold, rented, or shared with third parties or affiliates for their own marketing purposes.

5. How We Share Information

We do not sell or rent your personal information.

We may share information when necessary to provide the transportation services you request. This may include sharing relevant information with transportation partners, carriers, brokers, dealerships, or service providers involved in arranging or completing your transportation request.

6. Data Security

We take reasonable measures to protect the personal information submitted through our website and other communication channels. However, no method of electronic transmission or storage can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

7. Your Choices

You may choose not to provide optional information. You may also request assistance regarding your personal information by contacting us using the information below.

For SMS messages, you may opt out at any time by replying STOP.

8. Changes to This Privacy Policy

AVERO may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be posted on this page with an updated "Last Updated" date.
