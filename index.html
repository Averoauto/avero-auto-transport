Privacy Policy

Effective Date: September 9, 2026

1. Information We Collect

AVERO Auto Transport LLC may collect personal information such as your name, phone number, email address, pickup and delivery locations, vehicle information, and other information necessary to provide vehicle transportation services.

2. How We Use Personal Information

We use personal information to provide and coordinate vehicle transportation services, communicate with customers, brokers, dealerships, carriers, and transportation partners, schedule pickups and deliveries, provide customer support, and respond to inquiries.

3. Sharing of Personal Information

We may share necessary information with transportation carriers, brokers, dealerships, service providers, and other parties involved in providing requested transportation services.

SMS consent and mobile phone numbers will not be shared with third parties or affiliates for their own marketing purposes.

4. SMS Communications

If you provide your phone number and consent to receive SMS messages, AVERO may send messages related to vehicle transportation services, including scheduling, pickup and delivery updates, appointment confirmations, customer support, and transportation-related communications.

You may opt out of SMS communications at any time by replying STOP. For assistance, reply HELP.

5. Data Security

We take reasonable measures to protect personal information from unauthorized access, use, or disclosure.

6. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be posted on this page with an updated effective date.
